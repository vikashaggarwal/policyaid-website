Plan Highlights

Why ManipalCigna ProHealth Prime is worth a closer look

Unlimited Automatic Restoration

If your sum insured runs out during the policy year, ProHealth Prime restores it automatically and in full — with no cap on how many times this can happen in a year. This protects families against the worst-case scenario: a second major hospitalisation after the base cover is already exhausted.

Unlimited Restoration

Sum insured restores automatically, in full, an unlimited number of times per policy year — no single-restoration cap.

Worldwide Emergency Cover

Higher sum insured variants include cover for sudden medical emergencies while travelling outside India — useful for frequent international travellers.

OPD & Wellness Add-on

Optional cover for outpatient consultations, diagnostics and pharmacy bills, plus a wellness programme rewarding healthy habits at renewal.

No Room Rent Cap (Select Variants)

Higher-tier variants remove room rent sub-limits, avoiding proportionate deductions on the rest of your hospital bill.

No Claim Bonus

Sum insured increases for every claim-free year, rewarding healthy policyholders with growing cover at no extra premium.

541 Day-care Procedures

Full cover for all IRDAI-recognised day-care treatments that don't require 24-hour hospitalisation.

Maternity Cover (Add-on)

Maternity and new-born cover available as an optional add-on after the applicable waiting period.

Cashless Network

8,500+ network hospitals across India offering cashless admission, reducing upfront out-of-pocket payment at claim time.

Pros and Cons

An honest assessment of ManipalCigna ProHealth Prime

Advantages

  • Worldwide emergency cover on higher-tier variants

Disadvantages

  • Worldwide emergency cover restricted to higher sum insured tiers
