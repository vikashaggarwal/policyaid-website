An Honest Look at the Data

Travel Insurance Claim Settlement Ratio 2026 — What Data Actually Exists

Short answer: IRDAI doesn't publish a travel-specific claim settlement ratio per insurer the way it does for health, motor and life. Here's what's actually available, and how to evaluate a travel insurer properly instead.

9 Insurers Reviewed Data Gap Explained

Why There's No Official "Travel Insurance Claim Ratio" Table

Every credible comparison site — including ours, for the health and motor claim ratio pages — sources its numbers from IRDAI's Annual Report, which breaks out claim settlement ratio and incurred claim ratio by insurer for life, health, motor and fire insurance. Travel insurance isn't reported at that granularity — it's typically bundled inside a broader "Miscellaneous" or "Personal Accident and Travel" segment in regulatory filings, and IRDAI doesn't publish a standalone per-insurer travel claim ratio.

⚠️ If you see a specific "travel insurance claim settlement ratio" quoted for an individual company elsewhere online (e.g. "HDFC ERGO travel: 96%"), treat it with caution — it's very likely a general/health segment figure being repurposed, an insurer's own voluntary claim, or an estimate rather than an IRDAI-published number specific to travel. We'd rather tell you that plainly than publish a fabricated table.

What You Can Actually Check

General Insurer's Overall Standing

Every travel insurer in India is a general or health insurer's product line — check that parent company's overall claims reputation and IRDAI complaint ratio.

Cashless Network Abroad

Ask specifically whether the insurer has direct cashless tie-ups with hospitals at your destination, or whether you'll need to pay and claim reimbursement.

Claim Turnaround Time

Ask how long reimbursement typically takes for a straightforward claim — this varies far more between insurers than any published ratio would suggest.

24/7 Assistance Line

A round-the-clock, multilingual emergency assistance number matters more at 2am in a foreign hospital than any claim ratio ever will.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does IRDAI publish a claim settlement ratio for travel insurance by insurer?

No — not as a standalone published figure. IRDAI's Annual Report breaks out claim ratios for life, health, motor and fire insurance by insurer, but travel insurance is folded into a broader "Miscellaneous" or "Personal Accident and Travel" segment that isn't reported per insurer at the same granularity.

How do I evaluate a travel insurer without a claim ratio?

Check the insurer's global assistance network and cashless hospital tie-ups at your destination, their claim turnaround time, whether they offer a 24/7 multilingual helpline, and their overall company-wide claims reputation. These practical factors matter more day-to-day than a single ratio would.

Which insurers offer the most reliable travel insurance in India?

HDFC ERGO, ICICI Lombard, Bajaj Allianz, Tata AIG, Care Health and Niva Bupa are among India's most established travel insurance providers, each backed by a general or health insurer with a large, IRDAI-regulated claims operation.
