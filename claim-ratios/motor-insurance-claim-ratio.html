Reading These Numbers

Claim Settlement Ratio (CSR)

The percentage of claims settled out of all claims received, by count, in a given year. A 98% CSR means the insurer paid out on 98 of every 100 motor claims filed — a strong, direct signal of claims-paying reliability.

Incurred Claim Ratio (ICR)

Total claims paid divided by total premium collected, by value. The industry-wide motor ICR of ~85.51% means insurers collectively paid ₹85.51 in claims for every ₹100 collected in motor premium — a healthy range that reflects competitive but sustainable motor pricing across the market.

What to Check Beyond the Ratio

  • Garage network near you: A 99% claim ratio means little if the nearest cashless garage is 40km away.
  • Add-ons: Zero depreciation, engine protection and return-to-invoice cover often determine your actual payout more than a few points of claim ratio difference between two well-rated insurers.
  • Claims turnaround time: Some insurers now settle straightforward digital claims in minutes rather than days — worth asking about directly.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which motor insurance company has the highest claim settlement ratio?

Acko (99.98%) and Reliance General (99.32%) report the highest motor claim settlement ratios among private insurers for FY2024-25. Among public sector insurers, United India Insurance leads at 95.26%. The industry-wide average is 94.03%.

What is SBI General car insurance's claim settlement ratio?

SBI General reports a motor claim settlement ratio of 97.51% for FY2024-25 — above the industry average of 94.03%.

What is Royal Sundaram's claim settlement ratio for motor insurance?

Royal Sundaram reports a motor claim settlement ratio of 95.82% for FY2024-25 — above the industry average of 94.03%, though below the leading private insurers.

Where does this data come from?

Figures are sourced from IRDAI's Annual Report for FY2024-25. Always verify the latest IRDAI Annual Report before making a final decision, as ratios are revised annually.
